FORM — UI redesign concepts

Three directions for the gym logger, mocked with the same sample data (20 weeks of push/pull/legs training, daily bodyweight and Zone 2 cardio). Each board shows Today, the live workout, an exercise's progress and one secondary screen. All three share the same structural fixes; they differ in density, tone and how much they coach you.

Navigation is hub-and-spokeThe bottom bar is hidden, so every screen routes back through the six home tiles. Starting a workout takes three taps (Home → Workouts → Add workout).
The home screen is a launcher, not a dashboardThe API already returns the next-session recommendation, weekly sets per muscle, Zone 2 goal, streak and PRs. Home shows none of them.
Forms come before dataCardio opens with a 12-field entry form. Measurements opens with selects and buttons above the chart. History cards say “Imported workout · 2026-10-05” and show no volume or PRs.
Charts are hard to readY-axes use uneven ticks (114.9 / 137.1 / 159.3), PRs aren't marked, there's no hover readout, and Measurements puts weight and body fat on two different y-axes.
The logger is cluttered mid-set“Rest not set” separators, a photo row on every exercise and a sticky header that overlaps content. The last-session numbers aren't next to the inputs.
Visual language is inconsistentWeights range from 400 to 800, every section has an ALL-CAPS eyebrow, nav uses unicode glyphs (⌂ ◷ ▷), illustrated tiles sit beside line icons, and Videos uses its own style.

A · Pulse Dashboard-first · dark · evolves today's blue brand

For a data-driven lifter who wants everything at a glance. Today is a dense, scannable dashboard built from data the backend already computes: next session, weekly load, consistency heatmap, sets per muscle, Zone 2 and PRs. The logger follows the familiar Strong/Hevy table, with the previous session in its own column beside each input.
